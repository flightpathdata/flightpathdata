Your company or project could as well. And you can help make that happen.

# Custom Collateral

Partners who are able to drive awareness and help grow excitement for the Framework and tools bring a lot of value. In return we can provide customized materials in the form of tailored documents, graphics, slides, and more.

# Blog Exchange

The <a href="https://blog.csvpath.org">CsvPath blog</a> is active and growing. We are happy to place guest posts fitting the blog's quality, automation, governance, etc. themes, with the understanding that the editorial look & feel remains with us. Likewise, we are always happy to guest post where we fit your editorial calendar, or ad hoc on a topic we mutually benefit from.

# Support

We want you to be successful with CsvPath Framework tools. We are happy to support when you need it as a guest, presence, or trainer in media and during engagements, by remote, at your direction.

As time permits, we will be available for on the fly Q&A and support sessions. We do not have the team size or market position to support custom builds and extensions. However, if there is real urgency we will do whatever we can.

Give us a heads up at <a href='mailto:support@csvpath.org'>support@csvpath.org</a> so we can be ready.
